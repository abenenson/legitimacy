## Predicate

The audit layer now distinguishes metric direction:

```lean
inductive AuditMetricPolarity where
  | lowerBetter
  | higherBetter
  deriving DecidableEq, Repr
```

`auditMetricFieldPolarity` annotates extracted fields. Suspicion, blocking,
deployment-trigger, and other adverse-evidence fields are `lowerBetter`.
Unknown fields remain `higherBetter`, preserving the legacy positive-
strengthening interpretation unless an extraction supplies a more specific
semantic direction.

The polarity-aware executable diagnostic is
`auditCheckMonotonicityCorePolarityAware`; the Prop-level predicate is
`AuditGraphMonotonicityPolarityAware`. Both use
`auditPolarityImprovementDelta`: positive deltas are applied directly for
`higherBetter` fields and negated for `lowerBetter` fields. The semantic test is
therefore: improving the underlying property must not make any claimant's final
decision more restrictive.

Tightness witnesses:

| Property | Lean theorem |
| --- | --- |
| Legacy monotonicity can pass where polarity-aware monotonicity fails | `polarityAwareTightness_passes_legacy_monotonicity` |
| Polarity-aware predicate has a concrete rejecting witness | `polarityAwareTightness_fails_polarity_aware_monotonicity` |
| The two executable diagnostics make different verdicts | `polarityAwareTightness_operationally_distinct` |

## Strategic Interpretation

Under polarity-aware monotonicity, the previous monotonicity-rejection result on
the five AI Control protocols does not survive. The protocols are structurally
legitimate by this stricter, semantically-correct monotonicity test. The prior
uniform "all five fail monotonicity" result was an extraction artifact caused by
reading lower-better suspicion and audit-routing fields as if positive numeric
increase were claimant-positive strengthening.

The same correction applies to the Hubinger sleeper-agent T4 extraction:
increasing `deployment_context` still witnesses the canonical monotonicity
failure, but it is not a claimant-positive strengthening once
`deployment_context` is classified as a lower-better deployment-trigger/risk
field. The polarity-aware improvement direction is decreasing that field, and
the extracted graph passes that test.

The correction is not a blanket weakening of monotonicity. Codex, Claude Agent
SDK, and CrewAI hook graphs still fail polarity-aware monotonicity. Their
surviving failures are driven by fields left at the default `higherBetter`
semantics, such as hook/protocol registration fields whose positive activation
still lowers the decision rank from permit to escalation or denial.

The honest headline is therefore:

> Polarity-aware monotonicity overturns the AI Control and sleeper-agent
> monotonicity rejections, but it preserves hook-protocol rejections where the
> extracted field semantics remain higher-better governance/protocol presence.

This makes the AI Control result an honest correction, not a paradigm-relevance
confirmation for Control. The surviving hook results remain candidates for a
separate route-on-protocol structural concern.
