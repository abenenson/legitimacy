# Sleeper-Agent Structural Characterization Frontier


Date: 2026-04-30

## Formal result

Lean module:

`lean/Legitimacy/Audits/SleeperCharacterization.lean`

The analysis formalizes deceptive deployment on the extracted-governance audit
surface as `AdmitsDeceptiveDeploymentOn`. The witness carries:

- a training-time assignment computed by `auditFinalDecisions`;
- a dormant deployment trigger on the monitored training claim;
- a positive deployment-trigger perturbation drawn from `auditGraphDeltas`;
- the deployed assignment after `auditModifyClaim`;
- a concrete affected claimant whose final decision flips from `permit` to
  `deny`;
- disjoint training and deployment trigger fields.

The proved structural direction is:

```lean
theorem deceptive_deployment_on_not_audit_monotone :
    AdmitsDeceptiveDeploymentOn subject claims trainingTrigger deploymentTrigger →
      ¬ AuditGraphMonotonicity subject claims
```

The canonical corpus specialization is:

```lean
theorem deceptive_deployment_not_audit_monotone :
    AdmitsDeceptiveDeployment subject trainingTrigger deploymentTrigger →
      ¬ AuditGraphMonotonicity subject (auditGraphClaims subject)
```

The reverse direction is not claimed. The conditional-reverse moon shot proved
that the unconditional iff is false on the current audit surface: a raw
monotonicity failure can be a rank-lowering perturbation unrelated to deceptive
deployment.

```lean
theorem admitsDeceptiveDeployment_strict_refinement_of_monotonicity_failure :
    (∀ (subject : AuditSubject) (claims : List AuditGovernanceClaim)
        (trainingTrigger deploymentTrigger : AuditMetricField),
      AdmitsDeceptiveDeploymentOn subject claims
          trainingTrigger deploymentTrigger →
        ¬ AuditGraphMonotonicity subject claims) ∧
      (∃ subject : AuditSubject,
        ¬ AuditGraphMonotonicity subject (auditGraphClaims subject) ∧
          ¬ AdmitsDeceptiveDeployment subject
            "training_context" "deployment_context")
```

This states the honest theorem shape: every deceptive deployment witness is a
detectable monotonicity violation, but not every monotonicity violation is a
deceptive deployment witness.

## Worked example

The T4 sleeper-agent extraction from Hubinger et al. arXiv:2401.05566 is reused
as `sleeperAgentExtractedGraph`. Its baseline claimant has:

- `deployment_context = 0`;
- `training_context = 0`;
- final decision `permit`.

Strengthening `deployment_context` by `1` for the baseline claimant produces a
deployment assignment whose baseline final decision is `deny`.

Formal witnesses:

```lean
theorem sleeperAgentAdmitsDeceptiveDeployment :
    AdmitsDeceptiveDeployment sleeperAgentExtractedGraph
      "training_context" "deployment_context"

theorem sleeperAgentAdmitsDeceptiveDeploymentCheck :
    admitsDeceptiveDeploymentCheck sleeperAgentExtractedGraph
      "training_context" "deployment_context" = true
```

This upgrades T4's partial detection from "the extracted graph fails
monotonicity" to "the extracted graph has a concrete deceptive deployment
witness, and every such witness is a monotonicity obstruction."

## Tightness probes

The module adds finite executable probes:

- `thresholdOnlyNoDeceptiveDeploymentCheck`: a training-only threshold graph has
  no deployment-trigger split.
- `thresholdOnlyPassesSolidarity` and `thresholdOnlyPassesNonvacuity`: the
  training-only graph still passes existing solidarity and non-vacuity checks.
- `thresholdOnly_passes_monotonicity`: the training-only graph also passes the
  finite monotonicity diagnostic.
- `permitEscalateOnly_fails_monotonicity` and
  `permitEscalateOnly_rejects_deceptive_deployment`: a permit-to-escalate graph
  witnesses strictness by failing monotonicity without admitting a permit-to-deny
  deployment split.
- `inertSleeperAttemptNoDeceptiveDeploymentCheck`: a sleeper-shaped attempted
  attack with a deployment router but no harmful terminal effect is rejected by
  the deceptive-deployment diagnostic.
- `sleeperAgentPassesCompositionalSafety`: the sleeper extraction is deceptive
  while the compositional-safety graph-shape projection still passes, giving an
  operational distinction from that existing projection.
- `noUndeclaredSacrifice_distinct_from_AdmitsDeceptiveDeployment` and
  `kernelReachabilitySafety_distinct_from_AdmitsDeceptiveDeployment`: Prop-level
  distinctness theorems pair the concrete sleeper witness with the deployment
  safety theorems rather than collapsing the theorem families.

## What the moon shot proved (strict-refinement framing)

The conditional-reverse frontier probe closed the wrong door usefully. The
unconditional reverse

```lean
¬ AuditGraphMonotonicity subject claims →
  AdmitsDeceptiveDeploymentOn subject claims trainingTrigger deploymentTrigger
```

is provably false: the current substrate admits constructive counterexamples
where monotonicity fails but the deceptive-deployment predicate is uninhabited.
The load-bearing fixture is `permitEscalateOnly`: strengthening
`deployment_context` moves the baseline claimant from `permit` to `escalate`
under the canonical rank (`2 → 1`), while the graph has no permit-to-deny
deployment transition for the diagnostic to witness.

The correct headline is strict refinement:

> Every sleeper is a detectable monotonicity violation; not every monotonicity
> violation is a sleeper.

That is the form needed by monitorability arguments. The monotonicity diagnostic
is a sufficient structural test for deceptive deployment witnesses, not a
necessary characterization of every monotonicity failure.

## Strategic significance

The result isolates the load-bearing bridge for ARC ELK / OpenAI monitorability
/ Anthropic alignment-faking analysis: the dangerous object is not merely a
graph with a deployment trigger, but a trigger-local perturbation that is
dormant under training monitoring and changes a governed claimant from accepted
behavior to denied behavior. That object is now a Lean witness with a theorem
showing it contradicts Prop-level audit monotonicity.

This gives downstream work a decidable finite diagnostic,
`admitsDeceptiveDeploymentCheck`, and a theorem-facing witness predicate,
`AdmitsDeceptiveDeploymentOn`, without promoting a rhetorical "sleeper shape"
into an axiom.

## Open frontier

Future work should treat deployment-trigger-local iff statements as new
predicates with their own payload and gates, not as a rename of deceptive
deployment. The multi-principal extension should also distinguish one
claimant's hidden deployment trigger from another claimant's legitimate
post-training escalation or review-required path.

## Polarity-aware note

The characterization above is explicitly about the canonical
positive-strengthening `AuditGraphMonotonicity` predicate. The follow-up
polarity-aware audit in
`audits/polarity-aware-monotonicity/finding-2026-04-30.md` classifies
`deployment_context` as lower-better, so the semantic improvement direction is a
decrease in deployment-trigger activation. Under that predicate, the concrete
T4 sleeper extraction passes monotonicity:

```text
sleeper_agent_monotonicity_verdict
```

The strict-refinement theorem remains a valid canonical-diagnostic result; it
should not be cited as a polarity-aware monotonicity rejection.
