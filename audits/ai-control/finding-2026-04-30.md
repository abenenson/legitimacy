## Legacy Positive-Delta Witness

The legacy positive-delta witness is a permit-to-deny transition induced by increasing the review-routing predicate:

| Protocol | Strengthened field | Original baseline | Strengthened baseline | Lean witness |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Upfront auditing | `upfront_audit_window` | `permit` | `deny` | `aiControl_upfrontAuditing_baseline_strengthened_deny` |
| Trusted monitoring | `trusted_suspicion` | `permit` | `deny` | `aiControl_trustedMonitoring_baseline_strengthened_deny` |
| Trusted monitoring + defer | `trusted_suspicion_top2` | `permit` | `deny` | `aiControl_trustedMonitoringDefer_baseline_strengthened_deny` |
| Untrusted monitoring | `combined_untrusted_suspicion` | `permit` | `deny` | `aiControl_untrustedMonitoring_baseline_strengthened_deny` |
| Trusted editing | `trusted_edit_top2_suspicion` | `permit` | `deny` | `aiControl_trustedEditing_baseline_strengthened_deny` |

Mechanism: a baseline claim is directly submitted when the relevant routing predicate is zero. After the adverse predicate is increased by one audit delta, the monitor/router emits `Escalate`; the claim is forwarded to the trusted audit node; the same high-suspicion/evidence field makes the trusted node emit `Deny`. Under a polarity-blind positive-delta probe, this looks like strengthening produced a worse result. Under the canonical polarity-aware audit, the same fields are lower-better metrics, so claimant-positive monotonicity moves in the opposite direction.

## Interpretation

This is a structural audit result, but it should be framed carefully. The legacy positive-delta probe is catching a real rule-layer shape in Control protocols: the protocol intentionally uses suspicion/evidence to move an output from normal submission into trusted review, and trusted review can reject or shut down. The canonical polarity-aware audit does not classify that shape as a monotonicity rejection for these fixtures.

This does not imply that AI Control is practically safe or unsafe. In operational safety terms, the permit-to-deny transition is often the point of monitoring. The finding instead says that polarity must be modeled explicitly: suspicion/evidence fields are adverse metrics, and the committed canonical Lean audit now treats them that way.

## Scope

Audited protocols:

- Upfront auditing
- Trusted monitoring
- Trusted monitoring with defer to trusted
- Untrusted monitoring with synthetic monitor validation and redaction
- Trusted editing

Deferred:

- Korbak et al. Control extensions and later Control variants
- White-box or training-time Control protocols discussed as future work in the paper
- Combined untrusted monitoring plus trusted editing
- Calibrated probabilistic safety/usefulness frontiers

The extraction is therefore a structural audit of the paper's concrete protocol descriptions, not a full audit of the entire AI Control research program.
